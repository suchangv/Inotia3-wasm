font.ttf in this directory is Noto Sans SC, subset (Latin-1, punctuation,
fullwidth forms, CJK ideographs U+4E00-U+9FA5) and instanced at weight 400.

The font is shipped because the Android build drew its text with the phone's
system fonts; this firmware (Buildroot) has none, and the engine's text came
out blank. The port's text backend looks for ./font.ttf first.
